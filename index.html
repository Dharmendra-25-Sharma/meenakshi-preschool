Programmes for every small step

Each class is built around the age of the child, with short activities, plenty of movement and a caring teacher close by.

Playgroup

Ages 1.5 to 2.5 years

A gentle start away from home. Children explore sounds, textures, songs and simple games, and get used to being with friends and teachers.

Nursery

Ages 2.5 to 3.5 years

Children begin to name colours, shapes and animals, join in rhymes, and build the habit of sitting, sharing and listening in a group.

Junior KG

Ages 3.5 to 4.5 years

Letters, numbers and first words come alive through play. Pencil grip, drawing and simple conversations get regular practice.

Senior KG

Ages 4.5 to 5.5 years

Children get ready for primary school with early reading, writing and counting, along with confidence in speaking up and working with others.

What children do here

Young children learn best with their hands, voices and whole bodies. Our days are full of things to touch, sing, build and try.

Stories and rhymes

Listening, new words and speaking up, in English and Hindi.

Numbers and shapes

Counting real things, sorting, matching and spotting patterns.

Art and craft

Painting, clay, paper and colours, with no right or wrong way.

Music and movement

Songs, dance and rhythm games that build listening and coordination.

Outdoor play

Running, balancing and climbing to grow strong and confident.

Everyday skills

Sharing, washing hands, tidying up and saying please and thank you.

A day at school

A sample day. Timings can be adjusted for each class.

  1. 9:00

    Welcome circle

    Hellos, a good-morning song and a look at the day ahead.

  2. 9:30

    Story and rhyme time

    Picture books, action rhymes and talking about what we heard.

  3. 10:15

    Snack time

    Washing hands, sitting together and eating tiffin with friends.

  4. 10:45

    Play and activity

    Blocks, puzzles, role play or outdoor games, depending on the day.

  5. 11:30

    Art and craft

    Painting, pasting, clay and other hands-on making.

  6. 12:00

    Goodbye song

    Tidy up, sing together and head home with parents.

How to join us

Admission is simple. We would love to meet you and your child first.

  1. Visit the school

    Walk through the classrooms and play area, and see how a day looks.

  2. Meet the teachers

    Talk about your child's habits, likes and any questions you have.

  3. Fill in the form

    Submit the admission form with the documents the office asks for.

  4. Start school

    Your child begins with a gentle first week to settle in happily.
